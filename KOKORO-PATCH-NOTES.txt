WHAT TO DO NEXT
  1. Paste this over your folder / upload to a new branch.
  2. The WASM engine does NOT need rebuilding -- Kokoro is already
     compiled into sherpa-onnx v1.13.4. scripts/ is untouched.
     Either run "Build Read Aloud USB" in Actions, or if you already
     have dist/ReadAloudUSB, copy the four changed web/ files into
     shared/ and run ./scripts/build_launchers.sh (Go only).
  3. Download kokoro-en-v0_19.tar.bz2 from the sherpa-onnx
     "tts-models" release. Put model.onnx, tokens.txt and voices.bin
     into voices/kokoro-en-v0-19/ on the drive, together with the
     separate voice.json. Do not copy the archive's espeak-ng-data,
     README.md or LICENSE -- espeak is already baked into the engine
     and is byte-identical to the copy Kokoro ships.
  4. First load pulls a 330 MB model. A long pause is expected. The
     one real risk is an out-of-memory abort; if that happens, try
     Module.FS.unlink('/model.onnx') right after createOfflineTts().
  5. Confirm the ready message reports 11 speakers, then switch a US
     voice for a UK one and check they actually sound different.
     That last check is the one that catches a silent sid failure.
